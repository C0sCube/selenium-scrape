NEGATIVE COMPANY NEWS MONITORING – ARCHITECTURE NOTES
====================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Aggregate ALL negative company-related news in a rolling 24-hour format
with minimal maintenance cost and acceptable recall for compliance use cases
(e.g., NSE risk monitoring, adverse media detection, ESG/legal risk tracking).


PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
Traditional RSS aggregators (Google News RSS / Bing News RSS / Yahoo News RSS)
are NOT exhaustive news feeds.

They are:
    - Ranked retrieval systems
    - Top-K limited (typically 20–100 results)
    - Authority-biased
    - Regionally weighted
    - Volatile over time

This means:
    - Articles may be indexed by Google
    - But NOT retrievable via company-name queries
    - And may disappear from RSS after re-ranking

Example:
    Article appears in RSS at 08:00
    Drops out of Top-K at 10:00
    Still exists online
    But no longer retrievable via RSS

Hence:
    RSS = Sliding ranked window
    NOT a historical archive


RSS VOLATILITY
-------------
RSS feeds are ephemeral.
News that appears at time T1 may vanish from the feed at T2
because:
    - Newer articles published
    - Cluster merging
    - Ranking suppression
    - Regional relevance adjustments
    - Source authority weighting

Therefore:
    Fetch Once = Lose Forever

Solution:
    Poll-and-Persist model


POLLING BUCKET MODEL
--------------------
Instead of:
    500 companies × 120 keywords = 60,000 daily queries

Use:
    Company-only queries
    Every 2 hours

24h = 12 runs

Each run:
    500 companies × 1 query = 500 calls

Daily:
    500 × 12 = 6000 RSS calls/day

Persist ALL results returned in each run.

This creates:
    Union of 12 Top-K windows

Which approximates:
    Temporal Recall Expansion

Meaning:
    Even if an adverse article is ranked out later,
    it may have appeared briefly in an earlier polling window.

Negative news is typically:
    - Republished
    - Syndicated
    - Escalated to Tier-1 press

So capturing ANY credible coverage within 24h is sufficient
for regulatory monitoring.


MULTI-ENGINE SAMPLING
---------------------
Use:
    Google RSS
    Bing News RSS
    Yahoo News RSS

Each engine applies different ranking logic.

Union(Google ∪ Bing ∪ Yahoo)
    increases publisher diversity
    improves recall of:
        - Regional portals
        - Trade journals
        - ESG publications
        - Legal reporting sites


SOURCE FILTERING STRATEGY
-------------------------
Optional Tier-1 Query:

Query 1 (Open Universe):
    "Company Name" when:2h

Query 2 (Tier-1 Sources):
    "Company Name"
    (source:"Reuters" OR source:"Economic Times" OR source:"Business Standard")
    when:2h

Persist:
    Union(Query1 ∪ Query2)

Note:
    Source filtering increases precision
    but reduces recall.
    Hence should be used as secondary bucket.


GDELT – EXHAUSTIVE NEWS LAYER
-----------------------------
GDELT (Global Database of Events, Language and Tone)
provides:

    - Ingestion from ~65,000 global sources
    - Full-corpus querying
    - Historical stability
    - Keyword filtering
    - Built-in sentiment (Tone)

Unlike RSS:
    GDELT does NOT apply Top-K ranking suppression.

Sample API:

https://api.gdeltproject.org/api/v2/doc/doc
?query="Tata Steel"
&mode=ArtList
&timespan=24h
&maxrecords=100
&format=json

Tone range:
    -100 to +100

Filter negative:
    tone < -2

GDELT captures:
    - Trade journals
    - Legal portals
    - ESG watchdogs
    - Regional litigation coverage

Often missed by Google RSS.


FINAL DAILY PIPELINE
--------------------
Every 2 hours:
    Poll RSS (Google + Bing + Yahoo)
    Persist all results

Daily Aggregation (Rolling 24h):
    1. Filter Published >= NOW - 24h
    2. Deduplicate by URL
    3. Company/Alias match
    4. Keyword detection
    5. Sentiment classification
    6. Keep Negative news only

Optional:
    Query GDELT once daily
    Merge with RSS persistence


OUTCOME
-------
Provides:
    - Low-maintenance ingestion
    - Volatility mitigation
    - High probability adverse capture
    - Cost-efficient polling
    - 24h compliance-aligned reporting
